📋 Information We Collect

Quote Request Information (Customers)

When you request a free quote through the Tidyups Cleaning mobile app or website, we collect:

  • Required: Your name, phone number, and service type
  • Optional: Email address, property address, property type, number of bedrooms/bathrooms, preferred service date, and additional message

This information is used solely to:

  • Prepare an accurate quote for your cleaning service
  • Contact you about your quote and booking
  • Deliver and coordinate your cleaning service
  • Send important service updates and confirmations

Staff Location Information (Employees Only)

Cleaning staff members may optionally enable the "Cleaner Check-In" feature to share their live location while traveling to a job site.

  • How it works: Staff members manually tap "Start Sharing Location" and can stop at any time
  • When collected: Location data is only collected while the feature is actively enabled
  • Purpose: Used solely for dispatch coordination and scheduling optimization
  • Duration: Data is deleted once the service is complete
  • Customers are never location-tracked

Device Storage Information

The Tidyups mobile app stores small amounts of data locally on your device to improve your experience:

  • Your previously entered quote details (to speed up rebooking)
  • Your service preferences
  • Local app settings

This data remains only on your device and can be completely cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing your device storage.

🎯 How We Use Your Information

  • Service Delivery: To prepare quotes, schedule services, and communicate with you
  • Customer Support: To respond to inquiries and provide help
  • Service Improvement: To enhance our app and services (anonymized data only)
  • Legal Compliance: To meet tax, business, and regulatory requirements

✅ What We Don't Do

🚫 We do NOT:
  • Sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties
  • Use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking tools
  • Collect data from your device in the background
  • Share location data with third parties (staff locations are internal only)
  • Use your information for marketing without your consent
  • Store payment information (if applicable, handled by secure third-party processors)

🤝 Data Sharing

Your information is shared only with:

  • Tidyups Team Members: Cleaners and dispatch staff who prepare your quote and perform your service
  • Essential Service Providers:
    • Cloud hosting provider (for secure data storage)
    • SMS service provider (for appointment confirmations and alerts)
    • Payment processor (if applicable, PCI-compliant)

These service providers process data only on our behalf and are contractually required to maintain confidentiality and use your data solely for the services we request.

⏰ Data Retention & Your Rights

How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Quote Information: Retained for 2-3 years for service history and legal compliance
  • Location Data (Staff): Deleted immediately after service completion
  • Device Local Data: Remains on your device only; deleted if you uninstall the app

Your Data Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal information we hold about you
  • Correct: Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Delete: Request deletion of your personal information (except where legally required)
  • Opt-Out: Stop receiving promotional communications at any time
  • Withdraw Consent: For location sharing or other optional data collection

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

🔒 Security

We take data security seriously:

  • All data is transmitted over secure HTTPS connections
  • Sensitive information is encrypted both in transit and at rest
  • Access to personal information is restricted to authorized staff only
  • We regularly review and update our security practices
  • We comply with industry-standard data protection protocols

Note: While we implement strong security measures, no system is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and protect your account credentials.

🌍 International & Provincial Compliance

Tidyups Cleaning operates in Alberta, Canada. We comply with:

  • Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA): Alberta's provincial privacy law
  • Canadian Business Practices: Following industry standards for data protection
  • If collecting data from EU residents, we comply with GDPR requirements

📱 App-Specific Privacy Notes

iOS (Apple App Store)

The Tidyups app requests the following permissions:

  • Location (Always): Only for staff members using the Cleaner Check-In feature; customers' devices never request location permission
  • Notifications: To send you service updates and confirmations
  • Contacts (optional): To auto-fill your name if you grant permission

Android (Google Play Store)

Same permissions as iOS — location is optional and staff-only.

🔄 Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy as our services evolve or in response to legal changes. If we make significant changes, we'll notify you by email or through the app. Your continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my location tracked when I use the app as a customer?

No. Customers' locations are never tracked or collected. Location data is only collected from staff members who manually enable the Cleaner Check-In feature, and only for dispatch purposes.

Do you share my information with advertisers?

No. We never share your information with advertisers or marketing companies. We do not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics trackers.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. You can request complete deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us. We will delete your data (except where legally required to retain it) within 30 days.

What happens to my data if I cancel a service?

We keep your quote information for record-keeping and potential rebooking purposes. If you explicitly request deletion, we will remove it. Device-local data is yours to delete by uninstalling the app.

Do you use cookies or tracking pixels?

In the mobile app: No cookies or third-party trackers. Our website may use basic cookies for security and functionality, never for cross-site tracking.

How do I opt out of promotional emails?

Every promotional email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also contact us directly to stop receiving marketing communications while still receiving essential service updates.
